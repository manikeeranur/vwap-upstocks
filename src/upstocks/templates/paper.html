Paper & live trade — all four strategies

Market days 09:16–15:30 IST, every minute a few seconds after the candle closes, each switched-on strategy runs the same engine as the backtest on today's data so far. Paper only tracks the trades. Live (off by default) sends real Upstox intraday MARKET orders: BUY lots × lot size on a new entry (only if the signal is ≤ 3 min old), SELL when the strategy exits; open positions are always exited even if Live is switched off. Upstox accepts orders only from the static IP registered for your token. The scan runs inside the app — this page just shows it.
