Execution details

Active strategies scan every minute from 09:16 to 15:30 IST on weekdays, using completed candles. Paper mode tracks simulated trades. Live mode sends real Upstox intraday MARKET orders for lots × lot size, using signals no more than 3 minutes old. Open live positions are still exited if Live is switched off. Orders require the static IP registered with Upstox. The scanner continues while this app is running, even when this page is closed. Past-day replays do not place orders. Replaying today uses your current trading settings.
