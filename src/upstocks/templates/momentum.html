Rule parameters

The first run downloads daily prices for every NSE EQ company share (~2,350 Upstox requests; ETFs are excluded); Upstox allows about 2,000 every 30 minutes, so expect 30–45 minutes. Prices are cached, and later runs only fetch new days. While the market is open, downloads are slowed so the live scanner keeps its share of the API.
Uses stocks listed today — companies that delisted or merged are missing, which flatters past results (survivorship bias). Decisions use month-end closes; orders fill at the next open. Data runs up to yesterday's close.
